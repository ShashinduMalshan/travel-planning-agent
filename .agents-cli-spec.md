# Agent Spec: travel-planning-agent

## Overview
`travel-planning-agent` is an ADK-based multi-agent system designed to assist users in planning business trips. It processes trip parameters (origin, destination, duration, budget, travel purpose, and preferences) and coordinates specialist sub-agents to deliver flight options, hotel accommodations, day-by-day business itineraries, and a comprehensive budget breakdown.

## Example Use Cases
- **User Prompt**: "Plan a 3-day business trip from NYC to San Francisco for a tech conference next month with a $2,500 budget. Prefer non-stop flights and downtown hotels with high-speed Wi-Fi."
- **Expected Output**:
  - Flight options with departure/arrival times, airlines, and estimated pricing.
  - Hotel suggestions with nightly estimates and business amenities.
  - Practical day-by-day business itinerary with transit allowances.
  - Audited budget breakdown confirming cost stays within $2,500.

## Architecture & Sub-Agents
- **Travel Coordinator (`travel_coordinator`)**: Root agent orchestrating user interaction and delegating tasks to specialists.
- **Flight Specialist (`flight_specialist`)**: Identifies flight schedules, airlines, cabin classes, and estimated airfare.
- **Hotel Specialist (`hotel_specialist`)**: Recommends hotels based on business amenities, location, and nightly budget.
- **Itinerary Specialist (`itinerary_specialist`)**: Constructs structured daily schedules covering meetings, transit, and networking dinners.
- **Budget Specialist (`budget_specialist`)**: Aggregates all expenses (flights, hotel, per diem) and checks against the total budget.

## Tools Required (Prototype Initial Setup)
- `search_flights`: Mock flight search tool returning airlines, schedules, and estimated fares.
- `search_hotels`: Mock lodging tool returning hotel options, nightly rates, and business amenities.
- `calculate_trip_budget`: Computes itemized totals and verifies remaining budget margin.
- `get_business_destination_info`: Provides transit, business district, and dining guidelines.

## Constraints & Safety Rules
- **Price Estimates**: All prices must be clearly marked as estimates.
- **No Booking Claims**: Do not claim bookings or reservations are confirmed; state that prices and availability are subject to change.
- **Budget Awareness**: Keep recommendations practical and strictly warn if estimated costs exceed the user's allocated budget.

## Assumptions
- Uses mock tools/data for the initial prototype phase; Google Search grounding can be integrated in later phases.
- Built as a local prototype (`--prototype`); deployment targets (Agent Runtime/Cloud Run) deferred to later phases.
- No company RAG or document datastore needed for this initial phase.

## Success Criteria
- Accurately captures user requirements (origin, destination, dates, budget, purpose, preferences).
- Delivers realistic, well-organized estimates and itineraries without exceeding budget.
- Clean execution across ADK tool calls and agent transfers.
